# HLASM Bibliography

## Original papers

### IBM TR 00.1412-1 (Collier, 1966)
- **Citation:** William Collier, IBM Technical Report TR 00.1412-1,
  June 22, 1966. Title not known here; cited by number, as Collier cites it.
- **Notes:** The original description of testing successive switches with
  BXLE/BXH (see `IDIOMS.md`). Not known to be online. A machine-readable
  copy was asked for on ASSEMBLER-LIST and IBM-MAIN on 9 June 2024.
